HO-0521 ed. 06-24
LIMITS AND SUB-LIMITS ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: homeowners
Effective: 06-01-2024

This endorsement establishes special limits of liability. These limits do not
increase the Coverage A, B, C, or D limit shown in the Declarations; they cap
what we will pay within those limits for the categories listed.

SECTION I — SPECIAL LIMITS OF LIABILITY

A. Remediation of "fungi", wet or dry rot, or bacteria

Notwithstanding exclusion E-21 of HO-0304, we will pay up to $10,000 in the
aggregate per policy period for the remediation of "fungi", wet or dry rot, or
bacteria that results from a water loss that is otherwise covered under this
policy. This $10,000 aggregate is the most we will pay in a policy period
regardless of the number of occurrences, the number of locations involved, or
the number of claims made. The aggregate includes the cost of testing, air
quality monitoring, containment, removal, and post-remediation verification.
It does not reset upon renewal of the policy mid-term.

SECTION II — HOW THESE LIMITS APPLY

Deductible first. The policy deductible is applied to the loss before any
special limit in this endorsement is applied.

No stacking. Where an item falls within more than one category above, the
lowest applicable special limit governs. Special limits are not additive.

Relationship to exclusions. A special limit is not a grant of coverage for an
excluded cause of loss. Paragraph A above is the single exception, and it
operates only where the underlying water loss is itself covered.
