CONDITIONS AND DUTIES AFTER LOSS ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: homeowners
Effective: 01-01-2024

This endorsement replaces the SECTION I — CONDITIONS provisions of the
homeowners policy relating to the insured's duties following a loss.

SECTION I — DUTIES AFTER LOSS

In case of a loss to covered property, we have no duty to provide coverage
under this policy unless there has been full compliance with the following
duties.

A. Notice. Give prompt notice to us or our agent. Notice by any reasonable
means, including telephone or the claims portal, satisfies this paragraph.

B. Protect the property. Take reasonable steps to protect the property from
further damage. Reasonable expenses incurred for necessary emergency measures
are covered. Failure to mitigate does not void the claim in its entirety but
bars recovery for the additional damage that mitigation would have prevented.

C. Proof of loss. Send to us, within 60 days after our request, a signed and
sworn proof of loss setting out the time and cause of loss, the interest of all
insureds in the property, other insurance that may cover the loss, changes in
title or occupancy during the term of the policy, and specifications of any
damaged building. The 60-day period runs from the date of our written request
for a proof of loss, not from the date of loss itself, and not from the date
notice was given. We may extend the 60-day period in writing.

D. Records and examination. Show the damaged property, provide records and
documents we request, and submit to examination under oath while not in the
presence of any other insured.

E. Inventory. Prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing
quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss, attaching bills
and receipts where available.

SECTION II — SUIT AGAINST US

No action may be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with
all terms of this policy and the action is started within two years after the
date of loss.

SECTION III — APPRAISAL

If the insured and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either may demand an
appraisal. Each party selects a competent appraiser within 20 days of the
demand. The appraisers select an umpire. A written agreement signed by any two
of the three sets the amount of loss. Each party pays its own appraiser and
shares the umpire's cost equally. Demanding appraisal does not waive any of our
rights under the policy, including the right to deny the claim.
