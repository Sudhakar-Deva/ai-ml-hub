HO-0304 ed. 03-24
WATER DAMAGE AND SEEPAGE ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: homeowners
Effective: 03-01-2024

This endorsement amends SECTION I — PROPERTY COVERAGES of the homeowners
policy to which it is attached. All other terms, conditions and exclusions of
the policy remain unchanged.

SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS

The following exclusions are added. We do not insure for loss caused directly
or indirectly by any of the perils listed below, whether or not any other cause
or event contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. Terms shown
in quotation marks are defined in HO-0417.

| Code | Peril or cause of loss | Excluded? | Notes |
| ---- | ---------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| E-15 | Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water | Yes | No exception. Separate flood policy required. |
| E-16 | Water below the surface of the ground, including water that exerts pressure on a foundation | Yes | Includes hydrostatic pressure and seepage through a basement wall. |
| E-17 | Water damage resulting from the "sudden and accidental" discharge, rupture, or burst of a plumbing supply line within the dwelling | No | This exclusion does NOT apply. Direct physical damage caused by water escaping a supply line that ruptures suddenly is covered, subject to the deductible. The cost to tear out and replace the failed section of the supply line itself is not covered. |
| E-18 | Water damage resulting from freezing of a plumbing system while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied, unless heat was maintained or the system drained | Yes | Applies only during vacancy exceeding 30 consecutive days. |
| E-19 | Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water occurring over a period of 14 days or more from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system | Yes | The 14-day clock runs from the first escape of water, not from discovery. |
| E-20 | Water damage caused by the backup of sewers or drains, or the overflow of a sump or sump pump | Yes | May be bought back by separate endorsement. |
| E-21 | "Fungi", wet or dry rot, or bacteria, including any resulting cost of remediation, testing, or air quality monitoring | Yes | Applies even where the "fungi" results from an otherwise covered water loss. Limited remediation coverage is provided by HO-0521. |
| E-22 | Constant or repeated soaking of a roof, deck, or exterior wall by rain, sleet, or snow driven through an opening not created by a covered peril | Yes | An opening created by a covered peril is treated under the base wording. |

APPLICATION OF THESE EXCLUSIONS

Where a loss involves both an excluded cause and a covered cause, the
anti-concurrent-causation language in the paragraph above applies. Exclusion
E-19 is distinguished from E-17 by duration, not by the location of the leak: a
supply line that ruptures and floods a room in minutes is E-17 territory, while
the same line weeping behind a wall for three weeks falls under E-19.

SECTION I — CONDITIONS APPLICABLE TO THIS ENDORSEMENT

Reporting. The insured must report a water loss within 30 days of discovery.
Failure to report within that window does not by itself bar the claim, but it
shifts the burden of establishing the date of first escape of water to the
insured, which matters directly to the 14-day test in E-19.

Deductible. A single deductible applies per water loss occurrence regardless of
the number of exclusion codes considered in the adjustment.
