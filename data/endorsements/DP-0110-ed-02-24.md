DP-0110 ed. 02-24
WATER AND SEEPAGE EXCLUSION ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: dwelling_fire
Effective: 02-01-2024

This endorsement applies to the dwelling fire policy to which it is attached.
It does not apply to any owner-occupied residential form. Coverage under a
dwelling fire form is narrower than under a residential form, and the
exclusions below are applied without the exceptions found in residential
wording.

SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS

We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following.

| Code | Peril or cause of loss | Excluded? | Notes |
| ---- | ---------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| E-17 | Water damage from the discharge, rupture, or burst of a plumbing supply line within the dwelling | Yes | Unlike residential wording, no exception is made for a sudden rupture. All supply line water damage is excluded under this form. |
| E-19 | Seepage from a plumbing system, including any continuous or repeated leakage of water from a plumbing, heating, or air conditioning system, regardless of duration | Yes | No 14-day threshold applies under this form. Seepage from a plumbing system is excluded from the first day. |
| E-23 | Water damage occurring while the dwelling is tenant-occupied and the landlord has not maintained the plumbing system | Yes | Maintenance is a condition precedent to coverage under this form. |
| E-24 | Loss of rental income attributable to any excluded water cause | Yes | Follows the underlying property exclusion. |

APPLICATION

The absence of a duration threshold in E-19 is deliberate. A dwelling fire form
is written for non-owner-occupied risk where the insured is not present to
observe a developing leak, and the underwriting assumption is that seepage will
not be detected early. Producers should not represent this form as equivalent
to residential water coverage.

SECTION I — CONDITIONS

Inspection. We may inspect the plumbing system at any reasonable time. Refusal
of inspection suspends coverage for water causes until inspection is permitted.
