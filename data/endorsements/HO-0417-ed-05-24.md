HO-0417 ed. 05-24
DEFINITIONS ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: homeowners
Effective: 05-01-2024

This endorsement replaces the DEFINITIONS section of the homeowners policy.
Terms defined here are shown in quotation marks wherever they appear in the
policy or in any endorsement attached to it, including HO-0304.

SECTION A — DEFINITIONS

A. "Sudden and accidental"

"Sudden and accidental" means an event that is both abrupt in onset and
unexpected and unintended from the standpoint of the insured. Both elements
must be present. An event that develops gradually is not "sudden" even if its
consequences are discovered abruptly, and an event that the insured knew of and
allowed to continue is not "accidental" even if its onset was abrupt. The
discovery of damage is not the event; the escape of water, the rupture, or the
discharge is the event.

B. "Supply line"

"Supply line" means any pipe, tube, hose, or fitting that carries water under
system pressure to a fixture or appliance, including the flexible connector
between a shutoff valve and the fixture it serves. A "supply line" does not
include a drain, waste, or vent line, which carries water away from a fixture
and is not under system pressure.

C. "Fungi"

"Fungi" means any type or form of fungus, including mold, mildew, mycotoxins,
spores, scents, or byproducts produced or released by fungi. "Fungi" does not
include any fungi intended for consumption.

D. "Occurrence"

"Occurrence" means an accident, including continuous or repeated exposure to
substantially the same general harmful conditions, which results during the
policy period in direct physical loss to covered property. All damage arising
from one continuous escape of water is a single "occurrence" regardless of how
many rooms or building components are affected.

E. "Vacant"

"Vacant" means a dwelling in which substantially all personal property has been
removed and in which no person has resided for more than 30 consecutive days.
A dwelling that is furnished but temporarily unoccupied is not "vacant".

SECTION B — HOW THESE DEFINITIONS INTERACT WITH THE EXCLUSIONS

The exclusions in HO-0304 are scoped by these definitions. Whether a given
water loss falls inside exclusion E-17 or exclusion E-19 turns entirely on
whether the escape of water was "sudden and accidental" as defined in paragraph
A above. Adjusters must apply the definition before applying the exclusion
table, not after.
