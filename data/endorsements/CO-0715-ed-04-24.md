CO-0715 ed. 04-24
UNIT-OWNER LOSS ASSESSMENT ENDORSEMENT
Policy_line: condo
Effective: 04-01-2024

This endorsement applies only to a condominium unit-owners policy. It does not
apply to a homeowners or dwelling fire form.

SECTION I — LOSS ASSESSMENT COVERAGE

We will pay up to $50,000 for the insured's share of a loss assessment charged
during the policy period against the insured by a corporation or association of
property owners, when the assessment is made as a result of direct loss to
property owned collectively by all members, caused by a peril insured against
under SECTION I of this policy.

A $250 deductible applies to each loss assessment, regardless of the number of
assessments arising from a single loss to collectively owned property.

SECTION I — EXCLUSIONS APPLICABLE TO LOSS ASSESSMENT

| Code | Cause of assessment | Excluded? | Notes |
| ---- | ------------------- | --------- | ----- |
| E-31 | Assessment resulting from a deductible carried by the association | Yes | The association's master policy deductible is not an insured loss. Capped buy-back available by separate endorsement. |
| E-32 | Assessment resulting from earthquake or land shock waves | Yes | Regardless of whether the association's policy responds. |
| E-33 | Assessment resulting from water damage to collectively owned plumbing, where the association failed to maintain the system | Yes | Maintenance failure by the association is not a peril insured against. |
| E-34 | Assessment levied for capital improvements, reserves, or routine maintenance | Yes | An assessment must arise from direct physical loss to be covered. |

SECTION II — UNIT INTERIOR

Coverage for the interior of the unit, including fixtures, improvements, and
alterations made at the insured's expense, is provided under the base
unit-owners form and is not modified by this endorsement.
