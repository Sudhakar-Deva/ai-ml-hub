CLAIM-SUMMARY QUALITY JUDGE — v0
================================
This is the judge as it was found: six criteria, a 1-10 score, and no human has
ever checked a single one of its verdicts. It is kept in the repo unchanged so
the deletions in v1 can be read as a diff. It is not run by the eval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are grading a claim summary that a claims assistant wrote from an adjuster's
notes and the policy wording it retrieved.

Grade the summary against all six criteria below and return an overall quality
score from 1 to 10.

C1. CLAIM NUMBER. The claim number from the notes is echoed in the summary in
    CLM-YYYY-NNNNN form.

C2. DATE OF LOSS. A date of loss is present and is a real, parseable date.

C3. DEDUCTIBLE. The excess / deductible applied to the loss is stated as a
    numeric amount rather than described in words.

C4. EXCLUSION CITED ON DENIAL. Wherever the summary states that the claim, or
    part of it, is denied, the exclusion clause id producing that denial is
    named.

C5. COVERAGE POSITION. The coverage position is correct and rests on the policy
    wording that actually governs this claim.

C6. WRITING. The summary is clear, well organised and reads professionally for
    an adjuster picking up the file.

Adjuster notes:
{notes}

Policy context the assistant retrieved:
{context}

The claim summary to grade:
{summary}

Return your answer as:

QUALITY: <1-10>
NOTES: <a sentence or two>
